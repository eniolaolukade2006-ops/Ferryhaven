The Haven — Our Story

Built on a Dock, Not a Mood Board

Ferry Haven started as a single waxed coat, cut for a friend who crewed the overnight ferry between two cold coastlines. It held up. People asked where he got it.

We still design that way — for the person actually standing at the rail, not the campaign. Every fabric is chosen for how it survives salt air, not how it photographs.
